# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person managing their own finances, comfortable with backend development but new to frontend development.

## Product Purpose

Replace a personal spreadsheet with a simple way to track income and expenses.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered dashboards, spreadsheet-like density, and decorative interactions.

## Design Principles

- Make common tasks easy to find.
- Prefer familiar, predictable controls.
- Show financial information clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard use, visible focus, and readable contrast.
